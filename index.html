Organization Overview

Who We Care — Giving Voice to the Voiceless

Green Voice Global (NGO) was founded in 2001 with a solemn mission: to raise public awareness about environment, health, and education. We are uniquely managed and run as a trust by visually challenged individuals, proving that inner vision and boundless compassion can illuminate thousands of lives.

Remarkable Leadership

Guided by our Founder & Chairman, Mr. C.S. Veerraghavan (himself visually challenged), our team works relentlessly across rural Tamil Nadu and beyond.

Key Past Activities

Providing free regular dental check-ups, spectacles, vision screenings, and medical camps for disadvantaged children, mothers, and elderly citizens.

Future & Expansion Horizon

Empowering women through self-reliance, expanding child education programs, promoting renewable energy literacy, educating mothers on child nourishment, and extending free job-skills training to underprivileged youth, orphans, and differently-abled individuals (with specialized focus in Tamil, Hindi, and English).

Our Helps to Reduce / Core Activities

Camps & Grassroots Initiatives Done

We believe in direct action in the field. From classroom benches to village sapling fields and community clean-up drives, here is how we create measurable impact.

Education Camp

Education Camps & Student Mentorship

Organizing free evening tuitions, merit scholarships for marginalized children, and distributing comprehensive study materials (notebooks, bags, geometry sets). Specially structured psychological coaching to help students overcome exam fear.

  • Free tuition in remote villages & slums
  • Distribution of essential learning aids
  • Career orientation & exam fear eradication
Health Camps

Holistic Health & Medical Outreach

Conducting comprehensive health screenings including dental exams, vision testing with free eyeglasses, and preventive medical counsel. Dedicated doctor sessions guiding mothers to raise healthy, clean, disciplined, and nourished children.

  • Free dental & eye camps for public
  • Pediatric & maternal health advisory
  • Hygiene awareness & first-aid training
Waste Management

Waste Management with NCC Cadets

High-impact collaboration with National Cadet Corps (NCC) cadets to educate the public on segregation at source, plastic elimination, composting, and organized community clean-up drives across public premises and riverbanks.

  • Joint drives with NCC Cadets & youth
  • Public awareness on waste sorting
  • Cleanliness rallies across towns
Sustainable Development

Environment & Youth Employment Fairs

Tree planting drives in urban and rural stretches, rallies highlighting rainwater harvesting and global warming. Youth development capacity building and organizing employment fairs for unemployed youth in impoverished slum areas.

  • Thousands of saplings distributed & planted
  • Rainwater harvesting awareness rallies
  • Employment fairs for slum youth
